## ⭐ Featured Projects

<div align="center">

### 🎓 [ALIMS](https://github.com/basgenix4u/alims)
**The Global Academic Knowledge Infrastructure**
An institution-led registry for academic research records — theses, datasets, articles, and software — with verification, certificates, and discovery. Multi-tenant with PostgreSQL row-level security, Argon2id + TOTP MFA, a deny-by-default policy engine, and a hash-chained audit log.
<br>`TypeScript` · `NestJS` · `Next.js 15` · `PostgreSQL 16` · `Prisma` · `pnpm/Turborepo`

### 📊 [Excelite](https://github.com/basgenix4u/excelite)
**Turn an existing Excel workbook into a secure, multi-user web app**
Upload an `.xlsx`; keep the formulas and run them on a server. 150 Excel functions, **98.95%** measured fidelity, live collaboration over WebSockets, 16 chart types, five roles × eight actions, and an append-only audit log.
<br>`Python 3.13` · `FastAPI` · `Next.js 16` · `PostgreSQL 17` · `Redis` · `Celery`

### 🔥 [KILN-GUARD™](https://github.com/basgenix4u/KILN-GUARD-)
**Hybrid physics-informed predictive reliability for cement plants**
Raw vibration → features → Isolation Forest anomaly detection → XGBoost fault classification → physics validation → health index → risk score → RUL → prescriptive work order. Trained on real CWRU bearing data; physics self-checks against published ISO references.
<br>`Python` · `Dash` · `XGBoost` · `scikit-learn` · `Plotly`

### 🛡️ [PlagCheck NG](https://github.com/basgenix4u/plagcheck-ng)
**AI-powered plagiarism detection for Nigerian universities**
Detects verbatim copying, paraphrasing, and cross-language plagiarism (English ↔ Pidgin, Hausa, Yorùbá, Igbo, French). Offline-capable, NDPR-aligned, with NUC-style scoring policies and per-assignment overrides.
<br>`FastAPI` · `React 18` · `PostgreSQL` · `SQLAlchemy` · `Alembic`

### 🔐 [TrustVerify NG](https://github.com/basgenix4u/trustverify-ng)
**Verification-as-a-Service on a permissioned blockchain**
Issue, anchor, and instantly verify academic and professional credentials. Personal data never touches the ledger — only SHA-256 hashes, issuer identity, and ECDSA signatures — keeping it NDPA-compliant and tamper-evident.
<br>`Hyperledger Fabric 2.5` · `Node.js` · `React` · `PostgreSQL 16` · `IPFS`

### 🛒 [FUWMarketplace](https://github.com/basgenix4u/FUWMarketplace)
**A student-first marketplace for Federal University Wukari**
Buy, sell, request items, promote services, and find accommodation — with Google sign-in, Cloudinary uploads, admin moderation, and a notification workflow.
<br>`Next.js` · `TypeScript` · `Prisma` · `PostgreSQL` · `Tailwind`
🔗 [Live Demo](https://fuw-marketplace.vercel.app)

### ✍️ [AlimsWrite](https://github.com/basgenix4u/alimswrite)
**AI-assisted academic and business writing platform**
Topic generation, literature reviews, seminar and presentation generation, and verified research packs — with Paystack payments, Cloudflare R2 delivery, and a multi-provider AI router (OpenRouter, Gemini, Groq, OpenAI, Anthropic).
<br>`Next.js 15` · `Go` · `Python` · `Prisma` · `Cloudflare R2`
🔗 [Live](https://alimswrite.com)

### 🩺 [Medical Assistant](https://github.com/basgenix4u/medical-assistant)
**AI health information assistant with safety guardrails**
Symptom analysis, a natural-remedies library, and AI chat — every response labelled informational-only, with emergency-keyword escalation that short-circuits the LLM.
<br>`Next.js 16` · `React 19` · `Supabase` · `Groq (LLaMA 3.3)` · `Zod`
🔗 [Live Demo](https://medical-assistant-ashen.vercel.app)
